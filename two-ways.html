The Waters Within — Online Dance Immersion

Ideal for a winter reset, realigning from the body outward.

Originally experienced as a live journey shared with a circle of women, The Waters Within is an invitation to immerse yourself in the essence of water and its qualities through movement.

Across four interconnected classes, each building upon the previous one, we gradually explore the fluidity of the human body through softening, sensing, flowing and surrendering. A journey into the many ways water moves through us, inviting you to reconnect with your inner rhythms, awaken your natural expression and discover new dimensions of your own movement.
