The energy

Strong in softness

When I connect with The Water Devotion, I feel a fluid lightness. Even when we go deep into our own waters. We let the body settle into effortlessness. It can be easy. You can feel. Everything is allowed to be there.

We often live up in our heads. Here you arrive and ask: What is my truth in this moment? And within that truth, what is my greatest potential?

Water takes many forms. It moves from form into the formless.

What it gives you

What changes

  • Your body

    Your joints get room, your spine gets movement. Sometimes it's a hand resting somewhere and the question: What do I need right now? You take in what resonates, and you stop rushing.

  • Your relationships

    Less “you did it wrong”, more understanding and compassion. When you give yourself room for what wants to flow, you can offer that room to the person in front of you. We all want to be seen in our essence.

  • The now

    Raised shoulders soften again. You catch yourself holding your breath, and you exhale. How far can you actually exhale? Head, heart and belly connect, and you stop snapping back. You're more aware of what comes into your field, or no longer does.

  • Your work

    Your body sends signals you used to ignore so skilfully. Sometimes they point somewhere completely new. Sometimes you stay in your job and notice: it's about how you do it, not what.

  • Your expression

    Every emotion may move through you, unjudged. It can hurt sometimes, and you dance with it anyway. What's inside finds its way out.

Honestly

Why it isn't flowing yet

This doesn't happen overnight. It takes a little time and devotion. The more you move from “I must” to “I may”, the more this energy holds you.

  • I must, I must. Getting up every day and functioning, because the system says so.
  • Safety first. Deep down we all carry a gift for the world. For safety's sake, we lose it inside the system.
  • The head says: I can handle it. The body says something else, and the shoulders stay up.
  • Feelings get judged. Good, bad, too much. Instead of being allowed to flow.

Why water

Water stands for trust

Deep trust.

In Chinese medicine, water belongs to the kidneys. That picture places our essence there, the energy we're born with. Everyday energy comes from food, breath and sleep. The essence is nourished mostly by one thing: rest.

This is my own process too. I'm right in the middle of it, and I see its value. Dancing my way out of the system comes easily to me, and that's where I walk with you.

A traditional image from Chinese medicine, not a medical statement. This work is not medical treatment and doesn't replace a visit to your doctor.

About me

Hi, I'm Lucy

I see people clearly. What I see, I tell you straight, always to strengthen you, never as a mistake. When someone dances, I can't help but feel them and give them energy. Sometimes we just look each other in the eye and soften together.

In contact improvisation this is called trickster energy: a tap here, a tap there, oh, what does that do? That's what I bring into the room. Open up, try, move. Don't wait. Something happens through you.

Raised in Hamburg, living in Lucerne. I've danced since I was little, taught since I was 16, and found my way through freestyle and fluid movement to ZenThai Shiatsu.

Questions

Good to know

Do I need dance experience?

No, it's open level. If you do dance, you're just as welcome. The work is experimental and opens places you may never have felt before.

What about touch?

We approach it very slowly, never forced, following what your body longs for. You say what feels right and where you don't want to be touched. Mostly it's very gentle, up to giving me your whole weight, if and when you want to. Nothing is expected of you in the first session.

Online or in person?

Both. In the studio in Kriens (plus CHF 60 studio rent per session) or online.

What happens in the intro call?

A free video call you book straight in the calendar. We talk about your wishes, what your body longs for and which themes are alive. And we feel whether there's resonance, and in what frame.

Can I pay in instalments?

Yes, in 3 instalments.

German or English?

I speak both. We'll find your language in the intro call.
