My favorite place to dance is the ocean

As I let myself breathe and inhale the wind, the salt, the soft water molecules of the sea, I become one with the endless ocean inside me. Tapping into the fluidity of my body. Into one of the greatest forms of meditation there is.

Feeling the strength in the softness. The love and strength from my center. Realizing that the more I relax, the more it moves me. A constant fluidity.

The journey

Ever wondered what's underneath the surface?

Underneath your longing for expression and your individual way of movement? Ever wondered how it feels to embrace the fluidity in your body and in your rhythm of life?

I invite you to go on a journey within. Exploring layers that you meet each day but were never truly able to touch. A space of slowing down, to listen to your body, your breath and what wants to move through you.

Expression takes courage and patience. That's why we work with soft, intuitive methods, tailored specifically to you and your body. The pressure to perform is invited to leave. Your dance becomes softer, rounder, more authentic. And slowly, a deep trust in your inner flow and the mystique that lives within you begins to unfold.

You will grow braver in being seen, in your dance and in your life.

  • Unfold your inner flow
  • Feel empowered in your authentic body
  • Surrender to the softness of freedom, beyond achievement

Questions

Good to know

Do I need dance experience?

No, it's open level. If you do dance, you're just as welcome. The work is experimental and opens places you may never have felt before.

What about touch?

We approach it very slowly, never forced, following what your body longs for. You say what feels right and where you don't want to be touched. Mostly it's very gentle, up to giving me your whole weight, if and when you want to. Nothing is expected of you in the first session.

Online or in person?

Both. In the studio in Kriens (plus CHF 60 studio rent per session) or online. Online we work with movement, breath and my voice; touch happens in the studio.

What happens in the intro call?

A free video call. Just write me a short message and we find a time. We talk about your wishes, what your body longs for and which themes are alive. And we feel whether there's resonance, and in what frame.

Can I pay in instalments?

Yes, in 3 instalments.

German or English?

I speak both. We'll find your language in the intro call.
