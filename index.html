The Water Devotion

The art of becoming fluid and strong in your softness

I'm Lucy. I teach fluid dance and embodiment and give ZenThai Shiatsu, in 1:1 sessions and workshops, online and in Lucerne.

Explore the offerings The 1:1 with me

Who this is for

For those who dance not just to move, but to remember.

For dancers and movement seekers with an open heart.

You already know, or you sense, that everything lives in the body. And you are ready for change: for growth, for your creativity, for your own expression.

This is no escape from everyday life. It shows you how much your own daily practice matters, and how powerful it is to give yourself that time.

There is no goal. The dance is the way. In contact with others your horizon opens, and you see that everything is possible. It is one decision away, and it asks for devotion and commitment.

No dance experience needed, only curiosity about what is waiting to unfold.

I currently work 1:1 with women. Workshops and ZenThai Shiatsu are open to everyone.

Why devotion?

Devotion means centring in my heart

To me, devotion is surrender to something greater that sleeps within us, and it needs nourishing every day, because so much outside pulls us away. The Water Devotion is devotion to my inner being and to what I can touch within arm's reach; everything around it is circus.

When we begin to move, there is something beneath the surface that carries us, expanding through our joints and fascia, subtle yet alive. It asks for a willingness to explore, day after day, revealing hidden layers of movement, of breath, of expression.

Through dedicated practice, we begin to awaken the fluids within, softening into the process of opening and mobilizing. We sense how the body leads us deeper into its quiet, unfolding mystery, gently inviting our potential and intuition to emerge, again and again.

At times, it can feel challenging to surrender to this current. Yet for the curious, devotion becomes a doorway, an invitation to soften, to listen and to discover what longs to be felt and expressed.

We learn to meet the intensity of these inner waters with openness and ease, moving gradually – joint by joint, spiral by spiral, attuning to the rhythms that shape us.

May this devotion guide you into a dance of continuous exchange, where giving and receiving flow as one, where each inward turning deepens your presence and connection.

Water connects us all. The more we give ourselves to the greater whole, the more we feel that connection, and the illusion of being separate breaks. It is about remembering: What does my heart need? How does my heart speak?

Personally, from me

My favorite place to dance is the ocean

As I let myself breathe and inhale the wind, the salt, the soft water molecules of the sea, I become one with the endless ocean inside me. Tapping into the fluidity of my body. Into one of the greatest forms of meditation there is.

Feeling the strength in the softness. The love and strength from my center. Realizing that the more I relax, the more it moves me. A constant fluidity.

I invite you to dance with me and enter the ocean inside you. Letting go of all that was and becoming one with the eternal present, from where you are always creating. A space to explore your depth, your lightness, and to play with these two polarities.

About me

Hi, I'm Lucy

For a long time I thought I had to fight hard for everything. Now I'm learning to ask: what do I actually feel? What is really here? It's my own process too.

Raised in Hamburg, living in Lucerne. I've danced since I was little, taught since I was 16, and found my way through freestyle and fluid movement to ZenThai Shiatsu.

In the room I see people clearly and tell them what I see, always to strengthen them. My invitation: don't wait. Something happens through you.

Looking forward to flowing with you. Lucy
